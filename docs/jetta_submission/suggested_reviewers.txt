SUGGESTED REVIEWERS FOR JOURNAL OF ELECTRONIC TESTING (JETTA)
==============================================================

Reviewer 1:
-----------
Full Name: Prof. Andreas Veneris
Title: Professor
Department: The Edward S. Rogers Sr. Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering
Institution: University of Toronto
City/Country: Toronto, Canada
Email: veneris@eecg.utoronto.ca
Reason for Suggestion: Prof. Veneris is a pioneer of Boolean Satisfiability (SAT) based fault diagnosis, automated logic debugging, and formal verification in digital circuits (author of seminal IEEE TCAD and ICCAD publications). He is an internationally recognized authority on logic error repair and SAT-based EDA algorithms.

Full Name: Prof. Dr. Bernd Finkbeiner
Title: Professor / Faculty Member
Department: Faculty of Computer Science
Institution: CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security / Saarland University
City/Country: Saarbrücken, Germany
Email: finkbeiner@cispa.de
Reason for Suggestion: Prof. Finkbeiner is a leading researcher in formal methods, program synthesis, and neural circuit repair against formal specifications (author of ICLR 2023 works on iterative circuit repair).
